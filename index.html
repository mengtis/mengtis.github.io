Mengti Sun

Robotics Researcher

I build systems that let robots understand the physical world through contact as well as sight. My research fuses vision with contact-rich physics so that a robot can reconstruct the geometry, motion, and physical behaviour of objects it can only partly see — work published at Robotics: Science and Systems and developed in the DAIR Lab at Penn's GRASP Laboratory.

At Amazon Robotics I bring that research into production: designing multimodal data-collection pipelines and sensorized manipulator interfaces that capture human grasp demonstrations at scale for robot learning. Earlier, at UC Berkeley's Hybrid Robotics Lab, I developed the trajectory optimization that let an MIT Mini Cheetah autonomously jump through constrained openings. I continue to publish as an independent researcher alongside my engineering work.

Research Interests

Contact-Rich Perception

Fusing vision with contact dynamics to recover geometry, pose, and physical properties of objects under occlusion — without shape priors or fiducial markers.

Robot Learning & Manipulation

Multimodal data pipelines, sensorized grippers, and vision-language-action policies that let manipulation systems generalize beyond the objects they were trained on.

Motion Planning & Legged Locomotion

Trajectory optimization and real-time planning for dynamic maneuvers, deployed on hardware including the MIT Mini Cheetah quadruped.
